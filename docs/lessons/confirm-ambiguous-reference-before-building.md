# Confirm an ambiguous reference before building on it

**Summary:** When Sezgin points at something by position or pronoun ("das 2te", "das andere") and
more than one reading fits, ask which one — or show the candidates — before building.

## Where it comes from

Carried over from Chief Wants His Cut (and Hollowdeep). There, "mir hat das 2te am besten
gefallen" was read in the wrong ordering and a round was built on it; "das Menü schaut billig
aus" was answered with three ways to redo the main menu when he meant one grey window on the
screenshot he had just sent; an answer "1. passt 2. versteh ich nicht 3. …" was read against the
wrong one of two numbered lists in the message before it; "der Zeigefinger soll horizontal
bleiben" was built dead level when the fault was only that it stood on its head.

## How to apply

- **One numbered list to a message.** He answers by number and in few words: whatever he is to
  answer is the only thing that carries numbers; the rest is bullets or prose. An answer that
  fits two lists is asked back at once, with both readings named.
- **The fault he names is the measure, not the cure he names with it.** Build the smallest change
  that takes the fault away and keep the rest; say what the cure's word could mean beyond that.
- **A quotation that differs from what the game says is the message** — ask about that word first.
- A complaint that follows a screenshot is about what the screenshot shows.
- When a thing was changed and is disliked again, offer taking it out next to changing it once more.
- Verify a change through the path he actually uses ("Spiel starten.bat", F5), not only a dev path.
- Where a wrong reading costs one line of data, building on the likelier reading and naming it
  back is fine; where it costs a round, ask first.
