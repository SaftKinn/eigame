# Measure the difficulty with a tower like his, not a flat stand-in

**The lesson:** the tuning harness's fixed towers stand flat — shooters on the board, the egg in
the open. Sezgin builds compact and tall: shooters high up (range by height), the egg walled in.
The same gold buys him far more. Waves tuned until the flat towers lost "only just" were still
easy for him: he won all five without buying anything for wave 5.

**What happened (2026-10-24):** three tuning rounds aimed "wave 5 only just" at the flat towers
(115 / 250 / 390 gold). His screenshot showed a 245-gold tower that won with gold to spare.
Rebuilt in the harness (`--tower=his`) it won every run, no pest ever reaching it — while a flat
250-gold tower lost in wave 4. And a 180-gold flat tower kept "losing wave 3" through every
round: a sign the stand-in was weak, not the wave.

**How to apply:**
- When he sends a screenshot of a tower, rebuild it in the harness and make it a reference.
- Judge "too easy / too hard" against his kind of tower; keep the flat ones only to see that a
  careless build loses.
- Physics runs differ from each other: run each tower at least twice, and stop turning a number
  when a change shows nothing (a lower ant push, tried twice, changed nothing).
